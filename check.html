Project Report Summary

Student: Lakshya Sharma (2023348281) | Guide: Dr. Ravikant Nirala

The Silent Crisis

Menstruation remains a taboo subject in many Indian communities, leading to poor health outcomes. Our community connect project focused on Molarband Extension, New Delhi, revealed a critical gap between biological reality and social readiness. Women are suffering in silence due to myths, stigma, and a lack of accessible tools.

The Tracking Gap

65% of women surveyed rely on guesswork or memory to track their cycles. This leads to anxiety about "unexpected" dates and a lack of preparedness for symptoms.

The Digital Divide

While hardware access is high, software adoption is low. 80% of the target demographic owns a smartphone, yet culturally relevant health apps are virtually unused due to language barriers and complexity.

The RedPetal Solution

RedPetal bridges the gap by acting as a private, "floral" themed companion. It simplifies the user journey from anxiety to control.
